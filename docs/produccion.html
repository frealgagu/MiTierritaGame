Mi Tierrita production

How much every station of every zone produces, in units of its product per second, from level 1 to 100. Each cell shows the base value, below it the value with its ×2 research and, in gold, the coins per second if everything were sold. Data from v5.5.1.

production = (units per cycle ÷ cycle) × (1 + 15/99 × (level − 1)) × milestones

Each level adds 15/99 (≈ 0.1515). Milestones multiply: level 10 ×2, 25 ×2, 50 ×4, 75 ×4 and 100 ×8 (×512 in all). At level 100 the multiplier is ×8,192, and since cycles are powers of 2, every station ends at a power of 2 per second.

  • Stations in series. A station producing fewer units than the previous one is not a mismatch: its recipe asks for several. At the same level, each one consumes just what the previous one delivers.
  • Imports. What arrives by chiva, by truck (Bogotá) or by channel is marked in the recipe; the station does not produce more than what reaches it.
  • 💰/s. It is what selling everything the station makes with its research would give: useful for comparing, but what is really sold is what comes out of the end of each chain.
  • Costs. «Build» is what the station costs; «upgrade» is the base cost of raising it one level (it grows ×1.15 per level).
