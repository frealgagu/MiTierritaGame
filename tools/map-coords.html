Mi Tierrita coordinates

The zones and airports of each region on the game's map of Colombia, with a dotted latitude and longitude grid to locate the points that need fixing. Coordinates are in decimal degrees (latitude, longitude); west and south are negative.

Zones: latitude and longitude

Each zone stores the [latitude, longitude] of the town that inspires it in src/data/zoneGeo.js. The map converts them to its units (1/40 of a degree from 79.1° W and 12.6° N).

On the map they spread apart

When drawing a region, if two zones end up very close (for example San Gil and Barichara), they are pushed apart until their marks fit. That is why some are shifted from their town; the dotted red line shows it.

Airports

Since v4.4.10 each region stores the coordinate of a real airport near its last zone (AIRPORT_GEO, in the same file; approximate, to be checked). If it falls outside the region or on top of a zone, the map moves it; the dotted blue line shows it.

Everything inside its region

No zone or airport is drawn outside its region's departments or right on the border: if it falls outside, it moves to the nearest point of the region, a little inward. src/ui/mapLayout.js computes it, the same file the game uses.
