Privacy policy
Encantos de Mi Tierrita

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share anyone's personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your game is saved only on your device.

Who makes the game

Encantos de Mi Tierrita is an educational game made by an independent developer in Colombia (frealgagu). It works as an Android app, distributed on Google Play, and as a web page at frealgagu.github.io/MiTierritaGame.

What data it collects

None. The game does not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data. It does not use device or advertising identifiers, and it includes no third-party tools for advertising, statistics or tracking.

Where your game is saved

  • Your game and preferences (sound, music) are saved in the local storage of your device or browser. They never leave it.
  • If you use the backup in the menu, the game shows your game as a text for you to copy and paste on another device. The game does not send it anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing the browser data, deletes the game.

Internet connections

  • Google Play app: the game works without internet. It only connects, through Google Play, if an adult decides to make a voluntary payment (see below).
  • Web page: it is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about the visit, such as the IP address, according to its privacy statement. That log does not belong to the game and the game has no access to it.

Voluntary payments («Buy me a tinto»)

  • The Google Play app has an option to give a voluntary tip (a tinto, the Colombian black coffee, an arepa or a lunch). It gives no advantage in the game.
  • Before paying there is a question for adults, and on children's accounts with parental controls Google also asks the responsible adult for approval.
  • The payment is processed entirely by Google Play, according to Google's privacy policy. The game only receives the confirmation that the payment was made. It never receives your name, your email or your payment details.
  • On the web page, the tip, when available, is made on an external site (Ko-fi), which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is meant for all ages, including children. That is why it collects no personal data, has no ads, does not let you talk with other people, and puts payments behind a question for adults. This is consistent with Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012 (personal data protection), the United States COPPA law, and the Google Play Families policy.

App permissions

The Android app only asks for internet access (and to know whether there is a connection) and the Google Play billing permission, needed for the tips. It does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

For any question about this policy or the game, write to frealgagu@gmail.com or at github.com/frealgagu/MiTierritaGame/issues.
