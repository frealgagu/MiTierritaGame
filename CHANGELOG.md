# Changelog

## v5.21.0 — Carnival of Blacks and Whites
- **🎨 New season event: the Carnival of Blacks and Whites** (from 2 January, playable until 6 February): sew the costumes, paint the troupes black on the Day of Blacks, cover them in talc on the Day of Whites and bring them to the floats of the Great Parade. Its sticker is the first of the 🎉 Fiestas of Colombia album.
- **🎶 «Son del carnaval»:** an original son sureño with quena, charango, guitar and bombo drum plays in its square (also on the music download page).
- **The map dresses up for Pasto's carnival** from 2 to 7 January: bunting and streamers on every building and clouds of talc along the paths.

## v5.20.0 — Fiesta Square
- **🔽 Minimize:** the event bar's «Leave» button is now «Minimize», since the event keeps going while you play elsewhere.
- **Nights in the Fiesta Square:** the square has its own days and nights, so the candles, lanterns and Christmas lights glow (production never stops).
- **Its own buildings:** the Candle shop and the Corn mill each have their own look in the square.
- **🎶 Carols:** the Night of the Little Candles plays «Los peces en el río» and the Christmas novena plays «Tutaina», with flute, guitar, tiple, tambourine and maracas (also on the music download page).
- **Notice:** when a season event starts, the zone's person invites you to the square.
- **🎉 Fiestas of Colombia album:** each sticker now has its own illustration.

## v5.19.0 — Season events
- **🎉 Season events in the Fiesta Square:** they arrive with Colombia's fiestas, on their real dates, as the last section of 🏅 Events. First up: 🕯️ the Night of the Little Candles (from 6 December) and 🎄 the Christmas novena (from 16 December).
- **Long events with no medals:** about three hours of production, and they keep running while you play elsewhere. After the fiesta there are 30 more days to finish them; several can be pending at once.
- **🎉 Fiestas of Colombia album:** meeting the objective wins the fiesta's sticker, in a new album (Album › Fiestas of Colombia) with two pages of six and a 12-month calendar. A full page pays its Renown. They count for no achievement or the grand finale; each year the event comes back with no prize.
- **Fiestas:** Holy Week and the Flower Festival now only decorate (×1.10) and Love and Friendship Day is a big fiesta (×1.25): twelve big fiestas, nearly one a month.
- **Recommended upgrade:** a station that combines two branches no longer gets recommended when it is the other branch that falls short.

## v5.18.0 — Nothing goes to waste
- **🔥 The Furnace burns the bagasse:** the Cane Press's bagasse is now one of its inputs. Without bagasse it stops; at the same level it uses all of it, so the Sugar Mill sells only its panela.
- **🚌 New Compost route:** the Compost Bin's compost fertilizes the Cacao Grove, whose trees need it to give pods. The rest keeps the Farm's +30 % bonus.
- **Your game:** if the Cacao Grove is already open, you get the Compost Bin at the Wet Mill's level and the route at the cacao trees' level.

## v5.17.0 — Colombia's fiestas
- **🎉 Fiestas from the real calendar** (your phone's date, no permissions needed): Christmas lights, the Night of the Little Candles, Christmas, New Year's Eve, Epiphany, Carnivals, Holy Week, Mother's Day, Independence Day, the Battle of Boyacá, Halloween and more.
- **December dresses up every zone:** colored lights on every building, a Christmas tree, the manger (with the Three Wise Men on 6 January), candles and lanterns along the paths, the old-year doll and fireworks at night.
- **The people of each zone greet you** on fiesta days.
- **Fiesta bonus:** every region produces ×1.25 during the big fiestas and ×1.10 during the others, like a season (not in events). The 🕐 calendar shows today's fiesta or the next one.
- **💰 Income:** small multipliers show their decimals (summer ×1.2 used to read ×1).

## v5.15.0 — Byproducts are sold, and stations stop in events
- **💰 Byproducts are sold:** the second product of a station (costeño sour cream, coffee husk, pig skin, second-grade strawberries, salt blocks, chicharrón, rib broth…) used to be thrown away when no station used it. Now whatever is left over goes to the warehouse and is sold.
- **Pulp, bagasse and compost have a price:** pulp 1 💰, bagasse 5 💰 and compost 5 💰. The Compost Bin and the Furnace take their share first; only the rest is sold. A Compost Bin with a full yard no longer halts: it sells its spare compost.
- **⏹️ Stop stations in events too:** the switch now works on event boards, and a station stays stopped when you leave the event and come back.
- **Station panel:** the switch moved to the top, beside the close button, so long notices are no longer cut off. On small or sideways phones the figures no longer get cut or disappear.
- **🎉 Grand finale:** it has its own button beside Goals, Upgrades and Zones, instead of the one in the ⚙️ menu.
- **📊 Statistics:** the region list no longer closes by itself while you are choosing a region.

## v5.14.0 — Stop a station
- **⏹️ Stop a station:** in the station's panel, the switch beside its status stops or resumes its production. While stopped it makes nothing and its muleteers stay home; what it used to get goes to the other stations that use it or is sold at the warehouse. It stays stopped when you close the game and runs again when its region starts over.
- **You can see it:** ⏹️ over the building on the map, greyed out in the chain, and the stations after it and the goals that depend on it say so.

## v5.13.0 — The Coffee Region, a region like the others
- **New save format (10):** the save is now the whole game's and the Coffee Region is one more region, like the others. Restarting it only changes its own data; your profile, settings, events (slots, boards in play and rounds), the country clock and the other regions are left alone. Older saves convert by themselves when opened.
- **The country clock** has its own counter and never goes back to zero: it is the only one, and every region reads its date from it.
- **What a region keeps when it restarts** (its restarts, its completed ending, the second Willys, the chivas and the working hours) is now saved with your profile.
- **Backups:** a backup made with a newer version of the game says to update instead of opening half-way, and a newer save is never overwritten.

- **📊 Per region:** in the current run, each product counts what was sold up to 999Sp++ and what was produced up to 999Oc++.

## v3.7.0 — Events by slots and the player's offline hours
- **More spaced-out events.** Each region has **2 event slots** (the Eje Cafetero and Santander, each its own).
